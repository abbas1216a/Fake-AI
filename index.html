Advanced detection pipeline

Four complementary AI-powered systems detect manipulation across all media types.

01

🔍 Language forensics

Advanced NLP analysis detects sensational framing, AI-pattern markers (formal transitions, template phrases), missing source attribution, unusual punctuation stress, and structural oddities. Identifies GPT and LLM-generated text with 72%+ accuracy.

02

🎨 Image forensics

Byte-level entropy analysis, GAN residual detection, pixel anomaly scoring, EXIF metadata validation, and compression fingerprinting. Flags diffusion model outputs (Stable Diffusion, DALL-E) and face-swap composites with frequency-domain analysis.

03

👁️ Facial recognition

Facial landmark tracking, eye blink pattern analysis, micro-expression detection, and temporal jitter measurement. Identifies deepfakes by spotting unnatural eye movements and blinking irregularities across video frames.

04

🎬 Video temporal analysis

Lip-sync detection, audio-visual synchronization checks, frame interpolation artifacts, codec consistency validation, and temporal coherence scoring. Detects frame splicing and deepfake artifacts from tools like FaceSwap and DeepFaceLab.
